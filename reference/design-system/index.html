Rules
  • About 322 px wide, radius 10 px, 14 px from the window edge.
  • A scrim of about 35% black dims the page behind.
  • Header: name, DOB caption, close. Footer: secondary left, black primary right.
  • Slides in from the right in about 0.4 s.

The AI layer video

AI is a floating, temporary, violet layer. It never changes a result silently: the result appears as a normal status pill and a human presses the black button.

Voice

Principles
  • Short, declarative, sentence case, usually a full stop.
  • Benefit first, number close behind.
  • "X, not Y." Fragments in threes.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "Get started".
Headlines
  • ROI in months, not years.
  • Connect. Automate. Scale.
  • Patient access, automated.
  • Made by healthcare teams.
Calls to action
  • Request a demo (primary, everywhere)
  • Learn more · Our mission · Open Roles
  • Send Request (form)
  • Verb first, one primary per view.
